App lock

A PIN asked for when the app opens, and again after five minutes away.

Without the question a forgotten PIN would shut you out of your own book for good. The answer is kept as a hash, and matched with the case and spacing taken out.
This shuts the app, it does not encrypt it. What is stored on this device stays readable to anyone who opens the browser’s own tools — the PIN is for whoever picks up your phone, not for someone taking the device apart.
